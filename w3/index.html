Better Futures: A Reader's Guide to Utopian and Dystopian Speculative Fiction

Welcome to Better Futures, a reader's guide to the ways speculative fiction imagines, tests, and sometimes repairs possible futures.

We explore novels like Le Guin's Always Coming Home and Kim Stanley Robinson's The Ministry for the Future, asking how these books imagine alternative worlds and what they can teach us about the futures we might build.
